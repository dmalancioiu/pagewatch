# Reference UI Visual Notes

## Overall impression

The UI is a compact workflow-builder interface.

It feels:

- light
- quiet
- clean
- professional
- slightly technical
- dense but not chaotic
- mostly white and pale gray
- accented with soft blue/cyan

It does not feel playful, colorful, heavy, or marketing-like.

The whole interface is built around a central workspace, with navigation on the left and configuration on the right.

---

## Main layout

The screen uses a three-part layout:

```txt
Left sidebar | Central canvas | Right settings panel

There is also a thin top bar above the canvas.

The layout feels like a product builder, automation builder, or workflow editor.

The central canvas is the largest part of the screen.

The left sidebar is narrow and calm.

The right panel is narrow and dense, used for settings and form controls.

Left sidebar

The left sidebar is light gray / almost white.

It contains:

small logo at the top
quick search row
vertical navigation
nested navigation groups
plan/upgrade block near the bottom
footer links
user account row

The sidebar items are compact.

Each navigation row has:

tiny icon
small label
subtle spacing
light hover/active background

The active item is highlighted with a very pale blue background, not a strong color.

There is a tiny notification badge beside one item, using a small pink/red dot.

The nested items are indented and use very small icons.

The bottom “Free Plan” block is small and functional, not promotional.

The blue upgrade button is small and rectangular with rounded corners.

Top bar

The top bar is thin and minimal.

It includes:

a close/back icon at the left of the workspace area
breadcrumb text
workflow/page title
small “Draft” badge
right-side actions like notification toggle and publish button

The breadcrumb and title text are small.

The “Draft” badge is tiny and pale blue.

The publish button is the strongest blue element in the top bar.

The overall height of the top bar is compact.

Central canvas

The center area is a large pale workspace.

It uses a subtle dotted grid background.

The grid is barely visible and gives a builder/canvas feeling.

Most of the canvas is empty whitespace.

The main workflow content is centered.

There is a small icon block above the central heading.

The heading is small, dark, and semibold.

The subheading is smaller and muted.

The canvas uses lots of empty space, but the actual components inside it are compact.

This contrast is important:

spacious workspace
compact workflow nodes
Workflow nodes

The workflow blocks are small white cards.

They have:

thin borders
soft rounded corners
very subtle shadows
compact internal spacing

The first node is a small horizontal card.

It contains:

a tiny icon on the left
a short title
a muted subtitle

The main selected node is taller and more detailed.

It has:

pale cyan/blue border
white background
rounded corners
small header text
small metadata lines
compact key/value rows inside

The selected node border is one of the most distinctive parts of the design.

It is not bright blue. It is a soft cyan outline.

Connector lines

The workflow cards are connected with thin, faint lines.

The lines are light gray.

Small labels appear along the workflow path, such as step labels.

These labels are tiny and pale blue/cyan.

The connectors help the canvas feel structured without adding visual noise.

Right settings panel

The right panel is white and dense.

It contains:

title at top
tiny metadata row with icons/numbers
tabs
stacked form sections
inputs
toggles
dropdowns
option cards
bottom primary action button

The right panel is visually busier than the canvas, but still controlled.

It uses many small controls instead of large form fields.

Section labels are small and semibold.

Required fields use a tiny red asterisk.

Inputs are short, compact, and lightly bordered.

Selected/focused inputs use a soft cyan/blue border.

Tabs

The right panel has simple tabs.

The active tab is darker and slightly stronger.

The inactive tab is muted gray.

The tabs are small and sit close to the top panel content.

No large tab pills or heavy styling.

Inputs and controls

Inputs are compact.

They use:

white background
thin gray border
small text
small radius
tight padding

Dropdowns look similar to inputs.

Toggles are small and use blue when enabled.

Some rows have small plus icons or action icons on the right.

The controls feel utilitarian and precise.

Option cards

There is a two-option selector in the right panel.

Each option is a small card.

The cards have:

thin border
rounded corners
centered icon
small label

The selected card uses:

pale cyan border
very light cyan background
slightly stronger text/icon color

This is a good detail to reuse.

Buttons

Buttons are small and compact.

Primary buttons are blue.

Secondary buttons are white or very light with gray border.

Button text is small and medium-weight.

Corners are softly rounded.

No large pill buttons.

No gradient buttons.

No heavy shadows.

Badges

Badges are tiny.

They are used for:

draft state
notifications
small labels on the workflow path

Badge colors are soft:

pale blue
pale cyan
small red only for notification count

Badges should not dominate the UI.

Typography

The UI uses small typography everywhere.

There are no large dashboard headings.

Most text appears around:

tiny metadata size
small nav size
compact body size
small section title size

The font appears like a modern sans-serif.

The style is closer to Inter/system UI than a decorative font.

Font weight is restrained:

regular for most text
medium for nav/items
semibold for important labels/titles
Color feeling

The design is mostly neutral.

Visible color families:

white
off-white
pale gray
muted gray text
soft blue
soft cyan
tiny red/pink for notifications

The blue is used for primary action.

The cyan is used for selection and workflow emphasis.

The red is used sparingly.

The UI should stay mostly grayscale with small accents.

Borders and separation

The interface uses thin borders for structure.

Borders separate:

sidebar from canvas
right panel from canvas
cards from background
inputs from panel
selected workflow nodes from canvas

Borders are more important than shadows.

Most surfaces are flat.

Shadows

Shadows are very subtle.

Cards may have a slight lift, but nothing dramatic.

The design should not feel floating or glossy.

Use shadows only to gently separate workflow cards from the canvas.

Rounded corners

Corners are soft but not overly round.

Cards, inputs, buttons, and badges all use small radii.

The UI does not use huge rounded “bubble” cards.

Density

The interface is dense.

The sidebar has many items but remains readable because everything is small.

The right panel has many controls but works because spacing is tight and consistent.

The canvas balances the density by staying mostly empty.

This is a key part of the design:

dense controls on the sides
open canvas in the middle

Things this image does not use

Do not use:

large colorful stat cards
big headings
gradients
glass effects
playful emoji
cartoon illustrations
large icons
thick borders
heavy shadows
dark mode as the main identity
very saturated colors
oversized buttons
too much empty space inside panels
rounded 2xl cards everywhere
Identity summary

The identity is:

compact workflow tool
pale workspace
thin borders
small typography
subtle dotted canvas
left navigation
right inspector
soft blue/cyan accents
quiet professional SaaS

The main feeling to preserve:

A precise, lightweight builder interface.
```
